# 🎤 Pitch Coach — Innovators in Action

A university project where my team used **Design Thinking** to tackle a problem we'd all lived through: people with genuinely good ideas who struggle to communicate them when it's time to present.

We realized the issue wasn't just ugly slides. It was a mix of weak storytelling, poor visual design, little audience connection, low use of tech tools, and almost no space to get feedback before presenting. So instead of teaching "how to make pretty slides", we designed a platform to improve the *whole* preparation experience.

## 🔍 The Process

We used Design Thinking tools to understand the problem before jumping to solutions:

- **Forced priorities**, **experience maps** and **empathy maps** to dig into what users actually needed.
- We found the key needs were: better storytelling, stronger visual design, techniques to connect with the audience, modern tech tools, and adapting to different types of public.
- Main pain points: insufficient key skills, communication problems, information overload — and clear room for improvement.

The conclusion: improving the slides alone doesn't fix anything. You have to improve the entire presentation-prep experience.

## 💡 Ideation

After analyzing the problem, we generated several ideas and prioritized them using an **Impact–Difficulty Matrix**, keeping the ones with the most impact and least complexity:

- Group dynamics and prep spaces
- Reusable templates and resources
- Self-learning digital materials
- Simulations with personalized feedback
- An online forum for collaboration

The idea was to combine **learning, practice and collaboration** — not just teach theory.

## 📱 The Solution

A **collaborative digital platform** to improve presentations, aimed at students, entrepreneurs, and anyone who needs to prepare a talk. Its core functions:

- Help structure presentations
- Provide resources and templates
- Enable collaboration between users
- Offer support materials to build presentation skills
- Act as a prep space before the actual presentation

## 🎨 The Prototype

A **high-fidelity mobile prototype** with screens for: a welcome/intro, collaboration spaces, resources to learn how to present better, educational materials, and simple user-focused navigation. It was conceptual, but it showed how a real platform supporting effective presentations would work.
